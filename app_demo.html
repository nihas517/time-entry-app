1. Login Page

Every user starts here. Role and dashboard are determined automatically by credentials — no role dropdown needed.

2. Employee View

Employees see only their own data — they cannot see managers, admins, or other employees.

Log Time

Select a project, pick a date, enter hours, describe work, and submit for approval. AI flags entries over 10 hours or on weekends.

My Entries

All past entries with colour-coded status: APPROVED (green), SUBMITTED (orange), REJECTED (red), DRAFT (grey). Flagged entries show 🚩.

My Stats

Pie chart showing hours distribution across projects over the last 4 weeks.

3. Manager View

Managers see only their direct reports — Sarah sees Alice & Bob (Engineering), Tom sees Carol & David (Marketing).

Pending Approvals

Submitted entries as expandable cards. Managers can add a comment before approving or rejecting. Flagged entries show a warning banner.

Team Summary

Weekly snapshot of each direct report's total, approved, and pending hours. Zero-hour employees are highlighted.

Budget Status

Live progress bars for every active project — green under 80%, orange 80–99%, red over 100%.

Charts

Stacked bar chart of weekly hours by project plus a line chart of team hours trend over 4 weeks.

4. Admin View

The admin (Niha) has full access to all users, projects, and data across the entire organisation.

Manage Users

All users with name, email, role, department, active status, and username. Add new users with auto-generated credentials, reset passwords, deactivate accounts.

Manage Projects

View all projects with budget hours and status. Add new projects or close completed ones.

Full Report

Company-wide time entry data across all employees and departments. Downloadable as CSV.

🤖 AI Anomaly Detection

The system auto-flags suspicious entries in real time when saved.

RuleConditionFlag
Excessive HoursMore than 10 h in a single day⚠️ Excessive hours
Weekend EntryLogged on Saturday or Sunday⚠️ Weekend entry
High Daily TotalMore than 12 h total across entries on same day⚠️ High daily total

Flagged entries are visible to managers in Pending Approvals with a 🚩 warning banner.
